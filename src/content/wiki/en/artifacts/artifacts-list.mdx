## What Artifacts Are

Artifacts are the passive items you collect during a descent. Each one modifies your run — raw stats, weapon behavior, economy, or survival — but the real power comes from where you put them. Sephiria's inventory is a grid, and artifacts that sit next to each other reinforce one another. Placement is not housekeeping; it is the single biggest damage and survivability multiplier available to you.

## How the Inventory Grid Works

Every artifact you pick up occupies a cell in your inventory grid. When two related artifacts are adjacent — sharing an edge — their effects compound. A scattered inventory of strong artifacts loses to a carefully arranged one every time. Three rules of thumb:

1. **Cluster one theme per run.** If your build is about direct strikes, keep the artifacts that boost direct attacks in one connected block.
2. **Corner and edge cells are precious.** Artifacts in corners have fewer neighbors — reserve them for pieces that reinforce themselves or that you cannot connect yet.
3. **Plan before you place.** When you pick up an artifact, do not just drop it into the first empty cell. Hold it, look at the grid, and decide which cluster it belongs to.

## Artifact Categories

With more than 300 artifacts in the 1.0 release, most fall into a handful of practical families:

| Family | What It Does | Who Wants It |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offense | Direct damage, attack speed, crit, strike effects | Greatsword, Katana, Dagger builds |
| Zoning | Projectiles, area control, pierce | Staff, Crossbow builds |
| Defense | HP, armor, dodge, revives | Hard mode pushes, newer players |
| Economy | Gold, shop discounts, rerolls | Snowball and shop-centric runs |
| Synergy | Boosts adjacent artifacts, grid manipulation | Every build — always evaluate these |

Synergy artifacts deserve special attention: they make every neighboring piece stronger, so they anchor the most valuable real estate in your grid.

## Artifacts and Weapon Branches

Your weapon branch decides which artifact families pay off. A branch that boosts direct attacks compounds with offense artifacts; ranged branches get more from zoning pieces. Our [weapon upgrades and evolutions guide](/weapons/weapon-upgrades-evolutions) breaks down which branches amplify which effects, and the [best builds guide](/builds/best-builds) shows complete artifact sets arranged around proven synergies.

## Artifacts and Tablets

Tablets are the second half of the grid puzzle — around 70 of them in 1.0 — and they follow the same adjacency rules. A grid that reinforces artifacts on one side and tablets on the other is stronger than either alone. See the [tablets guide](/tablets/tablets-list) for how the two systems interlock.

## Quick Checklist for Every Run

- Pick a build theme before the first artifact.
- Keep each cluster connected — no orphan cells.
- Re-examine the grid after every shop visit.
- In co-op, trade duplicates so both grids stay themed.
