## What the Elru Quest Is

The Elru quest is one of Sephiria's NPC side quests — a hide-and-seek style task in which an NPC sends you hunting across three hiding spots spread through the tower: the carrot farm, the costume shop, and the ark puzzle room. It is a charming breather from the combat loop and a small tour of the game's more characterful setpieces, but the three spots are easy to miss if you do not know what you are walking into. Players who stall here usually assumed a lookalike floor was the quest floor, or searched the ark room before solving its puzzle.

This walkthrough covers how to get the quest moving, what to do at each of the three hiding spots in a sensible order, and what to do when a step does not trigger the way it seems to promise. Where the game's frequent patches may have shifted fine details since the 1.0 launch on July 31, 2026, the guidance below stays deliberately qualitative — if a step misbehaves, confirm current specifics against the official Discord or Kean's Sephiria Compendium.

## How to Start the Elru Quest

The quest begins with the NPC herself. Progress far enough into the tower that the themed floors and hub areas have opened up, then talk to Elru when you find her — quest NPCs advertise their business through dialogue, so speak to every new face the tower puts in your path, and re-check familiar faces between runs to see whether new dialogue has unlocked. Once the quest is accepted, your three objectives are fixed: the carrot farm, the costume shop, and the ark puzzle room.

| Hiding Spot | Floor Theme | What You Are Doing There |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carrot farm | The farm-themed area | Sweep the fields and search where a small hider could tuck away |
| Costume shop | The shop-themed area | Search among the displays, fittings, and shop furniture |
| Ark puzzle room | The puzzle area built around the ark | Solve or engage with the puzzle first, then search the room |

A note on order: the game does not force a sequence, so take the spots as the tower deals its floors to you. The only real ordering rule is internal to the ark puzzle room — puzzle first, search second — covered below.

## Hiding Spot 1: The Carrot Farm

The carrot farm is the most straightforward of the three. A game about a bunny descending a tower to save Bunnyville was always going to have a carrot farm, and the quest leans into the theme. Work through the field methodically rather than dashing across it: favor the places where a small body could plausibly hide — behind crops, along the field's edges, anywhere the scene builds a visual pocket. Interact with anything that highlights, and clear the area of enemies first so nothing interrupts the sweep.

If nothing triggers, the most likely explanation is that you are on a farm floor that simply is not the quest's farm. The tower is procedurally arranged, and floors sharing a theme recur across a run and across runs. Do not grind the same field for ten minutes — finish the run and take the next farm floor you are dealt. The quest spot will come around again.

## Hiding Spot 2: The Costume Shop

The costume shop plays the same tune with different scenery. Costume displays give a hider plenty to work with, so check behind and between the displays, around the fitting areas, and along the shop's edges before you conclude anything. As at the farm, talk to and interact with everything that responds — shop floors in Sephiria are dense with interactables, and the quest's trigger hides among the ordinary ones.

One extra step earns its keep here: exhaust the shopkeeper's dialogue if there is any. Side quests in games like this frequently gate their triggers behind talking to the nearby NPCs first, and the costume shop is exactly the kind of place where that pattern applies. Thirty seconds of dialogue is cheap insurance against a stalled quest.

## Hiding Spot 3: The Ark Puzzle Room

The ark puzzle room is the involved one, and it is where most players stall. This is a puzzle room first and a hiding spot second: engage with the puzzle's mechanics — observe the room, interact with the ark and anything the puzzle highlights, and work the objective to completion before you start hunting for Elru. Rushing straight to the corners of an unsolved puzzle room is the single most common way to waste a visit.

If the puzzle has you stuck, step out of the room and re-enter to reset your read on it, slow down, and check whether earlier steps actually completed. Puzzle rooms in roguelites are usually more observation than execution; the solution is generally visible in the room if you look at what the game is pointing at. And if the puzzle simply will not resolve, the troubleshooting table below has the escalation path.

## Troubleshooting the Quest

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cannot find Elru to start the quest | Not enough progression, or the NPC is not present this run | Progress deeper into the tower; re-check hub areas between runs |
| A hiding spot does not trigger | Wrong instance of a themed floor, or a sub-step skipped | Re-do the spot's sub-steps (dialogue, puzzle); otherwise finish the run and try another floor of that theme |
| Ark puzzle will not resolve | Puzzle step missed or misread | Leave and re-enter, re-observe the room, complete the puzzle fully before searching |
| Quest state seems stuck after a patch | Save or quest state shifted in the update | Verify game files through Steam; check the official Discord for known issues |

Two evergreen rules apply to all of these. First, patches have been frequent since launch — version 1.0.25 and beyond arrived within the first month — and live games occasionally hiccup on older saves after updates; verifying game files through Steam is the standard first aid. Second, when a step truly will not fire, the fastest answer is other players: the official Discord and r/sephiria respond quickly to quest questions, especially in the days right after a patch drops.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I have to finish all three hiding spots in one run?**

The safe approach is to finish the chain promptly while it is active. If you are forced out mid-quest, talk to Elru again on your next attempt to see where you stand — and confirm current persistence behavior in the official Discord if it matters to you, since details like this can shift between patches.

**Can I do the Elru quest in co-op?**

Quest content lives in the same tower in multiplayer, so a party can help you reach and search the themed floors. Remember that unlocks in Sephiria apply personally, so be the one to complete each hiding spot's trigger if it is your checklist that needs the credit.

**I searched the carrot farm thoroughly and found nothing. What now?**

Confirm the floor is actually the quest's farm and not a lookalike, clear it of enemies, and interact with every highlight. If it still does not fire, finish the run and take the next farm floor — procedural layouts mean another chance comes around quickly.

**Does the Elru quest count toward 100% completion?**

Side quests feed the game's broader completion picture alongside achievements and constellations. For the current patch's exact accounting, cross-check the achievements page or the community's full 100% achievements guide on Steam.
