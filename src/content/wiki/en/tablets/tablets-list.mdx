## What Tablets Are

Tablets are Sephiria's second grid system. You collect them during a descent — roughly 70 kinds exist in the 1.0 release — and each one strengthens a narrow slice of your run. Like artifacts, tablets care about adjacency: place them next to the right neighbors and their bonuses compound. An inventory that treats tablets as filler is leaving most of their value on the table.

## How Tablet Adjacency Works

A tablet's boost applies to what surrounds it. The practical consequences:

1. **Tablets feed artifacts.** The strongest grids put tablets against the artifact cluster they amplify — a tablet that boosts offense families belongs on the border of your offense block.
2. **Tablet-to-tablet chains are real.** Related tablets placed in a row reinforce each other, so a tablet strip can anchor an entire build theme.
3. **Placement beats count.** Seven well-arranged tablets outperform twelve scattered ones, especially in the late chapters where every multiplier matters.

## Tablets vs. Artifacts

| | Artifacts | Tablets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Count in 1.0 | 300+ | ~70 |
| Role | Define the build | Amplify the build |
| Best placement | Themed clusters | Borders of matching clusters |
| Flexibility | Commit early | Adjust as the run develops |

Artifacts decide what your run *is*; tablets decide how loud it gets. If you are still learning the artifact side, start with the [artifacts grid guide](/artifacts/artifacts-list).

## Placement Strategy by Build Type

- **Melee builds (Greatsword, Katana, Dagger):** line offense tablets along the edge of your weapon-artifact block so every swing benefits twice.
- **Ranged builds (Staff, Crossbow):** keep projectile and zoning tablets adjacent to each other, then border them with your zoning artifacts.
- **Hard mode pushes:** defense and economy tablets earn their cells — survival strips along the grid's outer edge keep your revive and armor pieces reinforcing.

## Co-op Note

In [4-player co-op](/co-op/co-op-guide), trading duplicates matters even more for tablets: a tablet that fits a teammate's grid is worth more there than as your fifth-priority piece.
