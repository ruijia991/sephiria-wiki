## How This Sephiria Weapons Tier List Works

Sephiria, the top-down action roguelite from TEAM HORAY (the studio behind Dungreed), reached version 1.0 on July 31, 2026, and the community meta has settled quickly around a clear hierarchy. This tier list reflects the consensus among experienced players as of the 1.0 patch, judged on four criteria: clearing speed, safety, how demanding a weapon is to play, and how well it scales into hard mode, which currently reaches at least Tier 35.

One important caveat before the rankings: every weapon in Sephiria can clear the standard tower. Tiers matter most once you push hard mode tiers or chase fast clears, where small differences in damage output and safety compound run after run. If you love a B-tier weapon, keep playing it; the gap is real but not run-ending.

A note beyond the six base weapons: shuriken-type pickups are rated very highly by the community. They are not permanent weapon slots, but when offered during a run they are almost always worth taking regardless of your build.

## S-Tier: Greatsword and Quarterstaff

### Greatsword — The Damage Benchmark

The Greatsword sits at the top of most tier lists for a simple reason: it has the highest raw damage among the base weapons, and its wide swings hit crowds as easily as single targets. In a game where rooms regularly surround you, the ability to cleave through multiple enemies in one committed swing shortens fights dramatically. That is also its weakness. Every swing is a commitment, and greedy extra attacks in boss fights will get you hit. Players who rhythm their swings between enemy attacks extract nearly full value from the weapon.

The Greatsword also anchors one of the most popular builds in the game, the Guardian Greatsword build, which leans into its big damage windows with a forgiving, defense-oriented setup. If you plan to push high hard mode tiers, this is the safest damage investment in the game.

### Quarterstaff (Staff) — Flexible Ranged Control

The Staff earns S-tier for a different reason: flexibility. It gives you ranged options and area control that melee weapons cannot match, letting you shape fights before enemies reach you. Zoning chokepoints, punishing approaching packs, and staying safe during boss patterns are all dramatically easier with the Staff in hand.

The trade-off is that the Staff rewards positioning more than reactions. You need to keep reading the room and repositioning, because the weapon is far less effective when enemies are already on top of you. In co-op, a Staff player who controls space while a partner deals burst damage is one of the strongest party configurations in the game.

## A-Tier: Katana and Crossbow

### Katana

The Katana is fast, precise, and extremely rewarding in the right hands, with a higher technical ceiling than the Greatsword. Its damage output is strong, and its speed lets you weave attacks into gaps that slower weapons cannot use. The catch is consistency: it demands tighter timing and better dodge discipline, and it punishes mistakes more harshly than the S-tier pair. Players who master it clear content extremely quickly, which keeps it firmly in A-tier and a favorite for experienced players chasing fast runs.

### Crossbow

The Crossbow provides dependable ranged damage from a safe mid-range distance, making it one of the most comfortable weapons for learning boss patterns. Its weakness appears in crowded rooms and against aggressive melee enemies, where its rate of fire struggles to keep pace with everything closing in. It pairs naturally with control-oriented talismans and freeze effects that keep enemies at range, which lifts it well above what its raw stats suggest.

## B-Tier: Sword and Shield and Dagger

### Sword and Shield

The Sword and Shield is the most forgiving weapon in Sephiria. Its defensive kit, anchored by the shield, gives new players room to make mistakes and learn enemy patterns without dying for every misread. That safety comes at the cost of a lower damage ceiling, which is why it drops in the rankings once you no longer need the training wheels. For a first clear of the tower, it is arguably the best pick in the game; for hard mode pushing, it falls behind.

### Dagger

The Dagger has the fastest attack speed of the six weapons and a satisfying, aggressive flow. The problem is reach. Its extremely short range forces you deep into danger for every hit, and in hard mode, where mistakes scale up sharply, that risk catches up with most players. It is the classic high-risk weapon: excellent in the hands of a specialist, underwhelming for everyone else.

## Recommendations by Context

| Context | First Pick | Why | Alternative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First run / beginner | Sword and Shield | Forgiving kit, room for errors | Greatsword |
| Solo clear speed | Greatsword | Highest raw damage, wide coverage | Katana |
| Co-op (support role) | Staff | Ranged control shapes fights for the team | Crossbow |
| Hard mode Tier 30+ | Greatsword | Big damage windows, proven build support | Staff |
| Comfort / pattern learning | Crossbow | Safe mid-range pressure | Sword and Shield |

The short version: start with Sword and Shield or Greatsword, graduate to the Greatsword or Staff as your main, and treat Katana and Dagger as specialist weapons for when you want a challenge.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best weapon in Sephiria right now?**

The community consensus for patch 1.0 places the Greatsword and the Quarterstaff (Staff) in S-tier. The Greatsword leads on raw damage and crowd coverage, while the Staff leads on flexible ranged control. Neither strictly outclasses the other; it depends on whether you prefer melee power or zoning.

**Does the tier list matter for a normal tower run?**

Not much. All six weapons are fully capable of clearing the standard tower. Tier differences become meaningful in hard mode, which scales to at least Tier 35, and when chasing fast clears.

**What weapon should a brand-new player use?**

Sword and Shield for maximum forgiveness, or Greatsword if you want to invest early in the weapon you will keep using. Both teach the game without punishing every mistake the way the Dagger or Katana can.

**Are shuriken pickups worth taking?**

Yes. Shuriken-type pickups are rated very highly by the community regardless of build. They are temporary rather than a permanent weapon slot, so grab them when they appear during a run.
